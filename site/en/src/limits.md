# What it cannot do yet

- **Meshes (STL, OBJ)** are not rounded or drilled as exact models.
- **Assemblies with joints:** several parts are possible (`add_part`), but moving joints through Claude are not there yet. They are made in the QymCAD window.
- **Text on a part** is not there through Claude yet. It is made in the QymCAD window.
- **Sketches** take points, lines, rectangles, circles, arcs, polygons and slots. Splines through Claude are not there yet.
- **The answers** Claude gets from the server are in English. It still talks to you in your language, and names menus the way your QymCAD shows them.
- **Claude in the window** works on macOS and Linux for now; on Windows only on a document of its own.
