# Tips for 3D printing (FDM)

| What | How much | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A hole for a screw | 0.2–0.4 mm wider than the screw (M3 → 3.4) | the plastic closes a hole in a little |
| A wall | no thinner than 3 nozzle widths (1.2 mm at a 0.4 nozzle) | a thinner one prints badly and breaks |
| Edges at the bottom (lying on the bed) | a **chamfer**, not a rounding | a rounding at the bottom is an overhang, and it sags |
| Vertical edges | round them freely | they print without trouble |
| Overhangs | no steeper than 45° | otherwise they need supports |
| The part on the bed | its largest flat face down | it holds better and needs fewer supports |

Before printing ask: "check that the part is sound, and show it from the top and from the side".
